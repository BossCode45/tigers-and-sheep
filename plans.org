** DONE Tiger bias in self-play
Before the fix, tigers won ~97% of self-play games and the net only reached ~15-20% against
=ClaudePlayer= at depth 3. 200 games each, tigers/sheep/draws:
| Players   | Random opening plies | eps  | Result     |
|-----------+----------------------+------+------------|
| Claude d3 |                   10 | 0    | 78/22/100  |
| Claude d3 |                   10 | 0.05 | 171/15/14  |
| Neural d3 |                    4 | 0    | 197/0/3    |
- Random sheep moves usually hang a sheep, so eps exploration alone tilts games to the tigers, and
  TD targets carried those wins back to the positions before the blunders.
- Fix: a position where a random move was played is labelled with its own search score, cutting
  the TD chain. Neural d3, 4 random plies, eps 0 became 126/4/70, and against =ClaudePlayer= (100
  games, depth 3) it went 12% at gen 5, 50% at gen 50, 50-62% at gens 60-100.
